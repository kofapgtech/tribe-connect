

# **1  Vision and first customer**

Starting point to confirm: one account to manage a Ghana visit, with the longer-term ambition to serve destinations across Africa.

## **What do I want Tribe Connect to make possible?**

Tribe Connect helps travelers explore their own curiosity by simplifying the trip management process, and visitors connect to like minded people during their journey. 

## **Who is the first customer I want to serve?**

* A first time visitor to Ghana who has heard about the country, but doesn’t know where to begin. They have found a hotel near Osu, but aren't the solo backpacking type. They’ve come to Ghana on an aspirational promise to reconnect with their roots. They use Tribe Connect because it's simple, trust worthy, and it allows them to modify their trip without penalty or difficulty. As they move around Accra and meet other visitors and locals, Tribe Connect helps adjust their plans as their knowledge of the city grows. Tribe Connect also protects its visitors. The full concierge support will advise guests of unsafe areas. 

## **What are their three biggest frustrations today?**

Their frustration is quality, trust and knowledge. Ghana has a large hospitality industry, but visitors come from cultures with a stronger sense of customer service. Traveling in Ghana can be stressful without support. Tribe Connect solves this by providing a strong, local, back of house team. 

## **Why would they choose us and return and What is my longer-term vision?**

Tribe Connect isn’t just about connecting people here in Ghana, its about keeping people connected once they return. Tribe Connect is a subsidiary of Diaspora Tribe & Trust. The vision of the organization is to build a cross border investing collective that extends beyond traditional ROI. The trust is about building a global tribe rooted in equity, learning, self determination and economic empowerment.
